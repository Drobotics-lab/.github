# JUIT Robotics Hub

The JUIT Robotics Hub is the primary technical organization at Jaypee University of Information Technology focused on the research and development of autonomous systems and hardware engineering.

---

### 🚀 Key Initiatives

* **Intelligence at the Edge:** Deploying advanced ML models on **Jetson Nano/Thor** for autonomous navigation.
* **Additive Manufacturing:** Utilizing **3D printing** for custom chassis and mechanical assemblies.
* **Competitive Robotics:** Developing high-performance bots for national-level track events.
